Our Work

Proven Results. Masterful Execution.

Every project is a story of challenges transformed into architectural triumphs. Here are two recent chapters.

Case Study 01 — Architecture & Construction

The Contemporary Mono-Block

The Challenge:

A narrow urban plot with limited natural light and high street noise.

The GrihoGori Strategy:

  • Inward Design: Created a central light-well (atrium) that pulls sun into every floor.
  • Acoustic Insulation: Utilized double-glazed windows and thermal-break frames to create a silent sanctuary.
  • Construction Rigor: Used a raft foundation to ensure stability on soft urban soil.

The Result:

A 30% increase in perceived space and a landmark residence in a crowded neighborhood.

Case Study 02 — Interior Design

The Minimalist Loft

The Challenge:

Transforming a cold, industrial shell into a warm, inviting living space.

The GrihoGori Strategy:

  • Material Fusion: Blending raw concrete with warm oak wood and fluted glass.
  • Smart Storage: Designed concealed, floor-to-ceiling cabinetry that acts as a wall.

The Result:

A clutter-free, high-end aesthetic that maximizes functionality.
