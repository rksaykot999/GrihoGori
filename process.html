The Master Builder’s Journey

From First Vision to Final Key.

We have eliminated the chaos of construction. Our integrated "Design-Build" workflow ensures that your project is delivered with surgical precision, financial transparency, and architectural soul.

01

Site Intelligence & Lifestyle Mapping

Before we design, we decode. We don't start with a drawing; we start with data. We perform a comprehensive "Site Intelligence" audit to understand the unique constraints and opportunities of your land.

Soil & Topography: We conduct geotechnical soil testing to determine the bearing capacity (qₐ), ensuring the foundation is engineered for a 100-year lifespan.

Climatic Analysis: We map solar paths and wind patterns to optimize natural cooling and lighting, reducing your future energy bills.

The Lifestyle Deep-Dive: We interview you to understand your daily rituals. Do you need a "silent zone" for deep work? A kitchen optimized for professional hosting? We map your life before we map your walls.

02

Architectural BIM & Digital Twinning

Experience your home before the first brick is laid. In 2026, we don't just provide "blueprints." We create a Digital Twin of your project using BIM (Building Information Modeling).

Clash Detection: Our software identifies if a plumbing pipe intersects with a structural beam digitally, so we never have to break finished walls on-site.

VR Immersion: You step into our VR Studio to walk through your home. You can test the height of the kitchen island and the view from your balcony in 1:1 scale.

Structural Rigor: Every cantilever and slab is calculated for seismic resilience using advanced stress-test simulations.

03

Procurement & Cost Certainty

No "Surprise" bills. No hidden costs. The biggest fear in construction is budget escalation. GrihoGori solves this through Strategic Procurement.

The Locked-In BOQ: We provide a comprehensive Bill of Quantities. Once signed, we utilize our warehouse network to lock in material prices for steel, cement, and finishing, shielding you from market volatility.

Material Transparency: You receive a "Material Passport" for your project, specifying the exact grades (e.g., Grade 60 Rebar, M-25 Concrete) and brands that will be used.

04

Construction & Subsurface Honesty

Engineering that lasts for generations. This is where the "Build" in Design-Build takes center stage. Our engineering team takes over the site with military-grade organization.

The Pour Audits: Before any concrete is poured, our structural engineers conduct a "Steel Audit" to verify the Reinforcement Area (Aₛ) against the design:

Aₛ = M / (φ · fᵧ · (d – a/2))

Daily Digital Logs: Every evening, you receive a push notification on your phone with high-res photos and a 360° time-lapse of the day’s progress.

Safety & Compliance: We maintain a "Zero-Incident" site with strict PPE protocols and environmental waste management.

05

Interior Finishing & Smart Integration

The transition from a structure to a sanctuary. As the "Grey Structure" reaches completion, our interior artisans and tech-integrators move in.

Precision Finishing: Our carpenters and masons work to a 5mm tolerance. Whether it’s book-matched marble or seamless cabinetry, the execution is flawless.

The Neural Network: We install your home’s smart-grid—integrating AI-driven climate control, biometric security, and circadian lighting systems that adjust based on the time of day.

Landscape Harmony: We execute the "Softscaping"—bringing in mature trees and vertical gardens to soften the architectural lines.

06

The 100-Point Audit & Handover

We don't just hand you the keys; we hand you a legacy. Before you move in, GrihoGori performs a rigorous Internal Quality Audit.

The Stress Test: We test all MEP (Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing) systems at 1.5x their working load to ensure zero leaks or failures.

The White Glove Clean: Your home is deep-cleaned and sanitized.

The Handover Dossier: You receive a digital folder containing all warranties, "as-built" drawings (showing exactly where every wire and pipe is located), and a customized maintenance manual.
