Knowledge base

Frequently Asked Questions

Technology & Innovation
Can I experience my design in Virtual Reality (VR) before construction begins?
Absolutely. In 2026, we believe seeing a 2D plan isn't enough. Every GrihoGori project includes a Full-Scale VR Immersion. You can put on a headset and walk through your future kitchen, check the light at 4:00 PM in your living room, and test furniture layouts before we ever pour the foundation. This prevents 95% of mid-construction change orders.
How does GrihoGori integrate AI into the design-build process?
We use AI-driven generative design to optimize for energy efficiency and structural load paths. During construction, we use AI-powered site monitoring to track material usage and worker safety in real-time. For the homeowner, we design "AI-Native" interiors—ensuring your home’s nervous system is ready for the latest automated climate, security, and lighting ecosystems.
Sustainability & Energy
Do you offer Net-Zero or Off-Grid construction solutions?
Yes. With energy costs rising in 2026, many of our clients are requesting Net-Zero homes. We utilize Passive Solar Design, high R-value insulation, and integrated Photo-Voltaic (PV) glass. We can design your home to produce as much energy as it consumes, incorporating Tesla-grade battery storage and rainwater harvesting as standard features.
How do you handle the carbon footprint of my building?
We provide a Carbon Impact Report with every structural design. By utilizing "Green Cement," recycled steel, and locally sourced materials, we aim to reduce the embodied carbon of our builds by up to 30% compared to traditional 2020-era construction methods.
Pricing & Logistics
How do you protect my budget from 2026 material price volatility?
We utilize a Strategic Procurement Model. Once the BOQ is finalized and the contract is signed, we lock in bulk prices for critical materials (Steel, Cement, and Finishing tiles) through our partner warehouse network. This shields you from the "market spikes" that often occur during a 12-month build cycle.
I am an expat living abroad. How can I supervise my project?
Most of our clients are global citizens. You will be assigned a Digital Project Dashboard. This gives you 24/7 access to live 360° site cameras, daily time-lapse updates, and a transparent log of all material deliveries. You can approve design changes or witness a "Slab Pour" from anywhere in the world.
Structural & Interior Health
What is "Healthy Building" technology, and do you use it?
In 2026, the "health" of a home is as important as its beauty. We implement MERV-13 air filtration systems and anti-microbial surfaces in high-traffic areas. Our interior consultancy also includes "Acoustic Engineering" to ensure that even in a noisy urban environment, your home remains a silent sanctuary (under 35 decibels).
How do your structures handle the extreme weather patterns seen lately?
Our engineering division now utilizes Climate-Resilient Modeling. We don't just build to the local code; we build for the 50-year weather cycle. This includes advanced "Thermal Break" windows to handle extreme heatwaves and reinforced foundation drainage systems to manage flash flooding.
The Turnkey Model
Why shouldn't I just hire a cheap local contractor and supervise them myself?
In 2026, construction has become too technical for amateur supervision. Between smart-home wiring, high-performance concrete additives, and complex municipal regulations, a "cheap" contractor can lead to legal fines and structural failures. At GrihoGori, we provide Professional Liability Insurance and a 10-Year Structural Warranty. We take the risk so you don't have to.
What is the "GrihoGori Post-Handover Support"?
Our relationship doesn't end when we hand you the keys. We provide a 12-Month Maintenance Period. If a hinge squeaks, a smart-light flickers, or a drain slows down, our "Rapid Response" team is at your door within 24 hours. We treat your home like a high-performance machine that needs an occasional tune-up.
